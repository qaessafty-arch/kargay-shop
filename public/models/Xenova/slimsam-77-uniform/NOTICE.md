# Floor-selection model

`onnx/*_quantized.onnx` are the 8-bit ONNX conversions (by Xenova, for Transformers.js) of
[nielsr/slimsam-77-uniform](https://huggingface.co/nielsr/slimsam-77-uniform): SlimSAM (Chen et al., 2023), a
compressed version of Segment Anything (Kirillov et al., Meta AI, 2023).
